Privacy Policy & App Usage Disclaimer
Published by Kushwanta Team Effective: November 20, 2026
Section 01

Our Privacy Commitment

Kushwanta is designed by the Kushwanta Team as a privacy-first personal finance and cognitive wellness application. This Privacy Policy explains what information is stored locally on your device, what limited information is processed if you choose to sign in, how third-party advertising partners operate, and how you can permanently delete your account and all data at any time.

If you have any questions about this policy, contact us anytime at kushwanta.team@gmail.com.

Section 02

Local-First Financial & Mind Arena Storage

Your financial records and game progress belong strictly to you. All personal finance data and cognitive game records are stored locally on your device using Android Room SQLite and encrypted/private app preferences:

  • Financial Ledgers: Transactions, accounts, budgets, savings goals, bill reminders, debts, metal card vault entries, warranties, quick presets, and calculator histories.
  • Mind Arena Progress: High scores, Arena XP, daily streaks, unlock states, and achievements across all 10 Mind Arena games.
  • Zero Data Selling: We never sell, rent, or upload your personal financial transactions or ledger records to external marketing servers.
Section 03

Optional Authentication (Firebase Auth)

You may use Kushwanta completely in Guest Mode without creating an account. If you optionally choose to sign in with Google Sign-In or Email & Password via Google Firebase Authentication, we collect and process only the minimal profile details needed to personalize your experience:

  • Display Name (to personalize your Profile Hub)
  • Email Address (for account authentication and password reset requests)
  • Profile Photo URL (when signing in with Google, to display your avatar in the app)

Passwords for Email/Password accounts are handled securely by Firebase Authentication and are never stored in plain text by Kushwanta.

Section 04

Third-Party Advertising Partners

To keep Kushwanta accessible and support ongoing development, the app integrates third-party advertising networks, specifically Google AdMob and Unity Ads.

These advertising partners may collect and use standard device signals—such as the Android Advertising ID (AAID), IP address, device model, OS version, and ad interaction metrics—to deliver, measure, and personalize advertisements in accordance with their respective privacy policies. You can reset or limit ad personalization at any time in your Android device's Settings → Google → Ads menu.

Section 05

One-Tap Account & Data Deletion

You have complete control over your account and data. You can permanently delete your linked account and wipe every local record directly inside the app at any time:

Open Settings → Data & Reset Controls → Delete Account & All Data. Confirming this action permanently deletes your active Firebase Auth user account (if signed in) and erases all local Room database tables, preferences, avatar files, and Mind Arena game progress from your device.

Alternatively, you may request account deletion assistance by emailing kushwanta.team@gmail.com from your registered email address. Note that using Sign Out in Profile only ends your active login session and preserves your local vault data.

Section 06

Children's Privacy

Kushwanta is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or the minimum digital consent age in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us immediately at kushwanta.team@gmail.com so we can promptly remove it.
